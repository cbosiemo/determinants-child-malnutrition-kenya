# Determinants of Child Malnutrition in Kenya

## Overview

This repository contains the code, documentation, and outputs for a quantitative research project examining the determinants of child malnutrition among children under five years of age in Kenya using data from the 2022 Kenya Demographic and Health Survey (KDHS).

The project demonstrates an end-to-end research workflow, including:

- Research design
- Data management and cleaning
- Exploratory data analysis
- Statistical analysis using Stata
- Interpretation of findings
- Research reporting

## Research Question

What child, maternal, and household factors are associated with child malnutrition among children under five years in Kenya?

## Dataset

Kenya Demographic and Health Survey (KDHS) 2022.

The raw DHS data are **not included** in this repository because they require permission from The DHS Program.

Researchers can request access here:

https://dhsprogram.com
